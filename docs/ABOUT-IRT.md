# Project background and IRT

[Home](../README.md) · [Русский](#русский)

RF CALC was created by Timur Gabzalilov, a design engineer at IRT, for the company's antenna-engineering needs and at its request. This relationship is described by the project author; the public company website is the source for the company information below.

IRT develops and manufactures UAVs and associated hardware/software in Ufa, Russia, including agricultural drones. The company's website describes local manufacturing, a design office and software development capabilities.
